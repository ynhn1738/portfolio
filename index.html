Case 01 · Data & operations platform

Half the "wrong numbers" were not report bugs

Mantu · MARS reportingProduct Owner · 2023 – now
What I achieved 82%
  • 88 of 107 targeted data problems fixed by the foundation I pushed for
  • 31% of all user feedback resolved in one release
  • 96% of MARS-side errors fixed across 408,865 automated checks
Insight

I classified 217 manager complaints. 49% came from source data changed after the fact, not from MARS. 57% of people changes and 55% of project changes were backdated, but MARS froze each week.

What I did

Reframed the roadmap from patching rules to rebuilding data history, so any past week can be recomputed. Set ownership across 9 source teams: they own the data, MARS owns the rules.

Result

Foundation v1 fixes 88 of 107 targeted problems, and 31% of all user feedback in one release. Automated checks across 635 managers show 96% of MARS-side errors fixed.

Case 02 · Workflow & risk product

Contract review inside the sales flow, shaped with its users

Mantu · Opportunity ManagementProduct lead · discovery to MVP
What I achieved 3 roles
  • Ran discovery workshops with senior business and legal users, including directors, and got Sales and Legal to agree on one review flow
  • Turned 30 pains into 18 user stories and a controlled MVP that everyone signed up to, with a measurable north star
  • Tested a prototype built with AI in weeks; 2 design changes came straight from usability sessions
Journey mapping→User story mapping→Rapid prototype→Usability testing
Discovery

Users worked around the legal tool in email and chat, so its data could not show the real process. I chose to go to the people instead: I facilitated journey-mapping workshops with a representative of each persona, across 7 stages from draft to signed contract. They surfaced 30 pains against only 3 things that worked.

Alignment

Sales wanted speed, Legal wanted control, directors needed to know what they were approving. In a story-mapping workshop with the same senior users, I turned those needs into shared priorities: 18 stories, a controlled MVP, then optimise and scale. Agreed non-goals kept scope tight.

Validation

I built the prototype with AI tools: risk-based routing to Legal or Director, 26 commitment rules, controlled bypass. Testing with a manager and a sales director moved live editing out of the MVP and added a rules checklist for directors.

Case 04 · Payments

A failed A/B test, read correctly, became a 72% success rate

MoMo · cash-in flowProduct Manager · 2023
What I achieved 25→72%
  • D-OTP cash-in success nearly tripled after one targeted UX fix
  • Saved the D-OTP rollout for MB Bank cash-in instead of rolling it back
Context

I led the switch from SMS OTP to digital OTP (D-OTP) for cash-in from MB Bank to the MoMo wallet. The first A/B test showed only 25% of D-OTP attempts succeeding.

Insight

The new flow worked technically. The gap was UX: the dialog for D-OTP looked almost the same as the old one telling users to open their SMS, so users waited for a text that never came.

Result

I shipped a clearer step that tells users to open the MB Bank app to get their OTP. Success rate rose from 25% to 72%, and the D-OTP rollout went ahead.
